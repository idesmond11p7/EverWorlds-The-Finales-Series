# EverWorlds: The Finales — Project Bible

## Status

**Living document.** This records confirmed understanding. Provisional ideas must not be treated as finalized architecture or requirements.

## 1. EverWorlds Series: The Finales

EverWorlds: The Finales is envisioned as a powerful, large-scale React-based extension for SillyTavern.

It is an extension that heavily modifies SillyTavern's properties and behavior. It also adds extra features and capabilities and has a specific internal roleplay structure.

The internal mechanics are not yet fully defined. This document therefore avoids inventing architecture or implementation details that have not been established.

## 2. Core Purpose

The true/core goal of EverWorlds is to solve basic and fundamental LLM issues that interfere with high-quality roleplay.

The project is highly aware of LLM limitations, tendencies, and failure patterns. It is intended to actively prevent or mitigate those problems so they do not degrade roleplay quality.

## 3. Quality Standard

EverWorlds is intended to be professional-tier.

Quality, consistency, coherence, realism, and disciplined production are therefore treated as project-level concerns rather than optional polish.

## 4. Digital Twins

The series produced by the project are usually envisioned as highly detailed digital twins of a world, a person, or another subject.

These outputs are intended to be highly detailed and psychologically realistic.

## 5. The Finale Concept

A **Finale** is usually a highly evolved version of an existing world.

A Finale is known for being substantially better than the original world while retaining its original identity and defining traits.

The transformation is deep: the resulting world may be vastly different in quality, depth, sophistication, and complexity while still being recognizably the same world.

### Core principle

**Preservation + Evolution**

- **Preservation:** retain the original world's identity, defining traits, and foundational characteristics.
- **Evolution:** deeply improve weaknesses, deepen systems, increase psychological realism, improve consistency, and introduce sophisticated structures and capabilities where appropriate.

A Finale is therefore not simply a polished or upgraded version. It is a deep evolution that preserves identity while becoming unrecognizably better.

## 6. Project Productivity Goal

The Scheme of Work is intended as a project productivity and production framework, not a school or teaching document.

Its purpose is to help the project reach its end goal without emotional burnout, disorder, messes, inconsistencies, or uncontrolled work interfering with progress.
