## 1. Naming and Scope

- **“Your verse”** is the working label for the broader cosmological structure.
- **Forever Worlds** is a fantasy story/setting name and inspiration; it is not the name for the entire cosmology.

## 2. Creation Is Not the Beginning of Everything

God created Heaven, Earth, humanity, and races intrinsically linked to humanity. That creation event is not the beginning of all creation.

The existence of Heaven, Earth, humanity, and their related races does not establish that they exhaust all existence. The broader cosmos extends beyond that creation event.

**Not established by this note:** whether that God created every realm, force, or form of existence. Do not infer an answer without an explicit source.

## 3. Realms Do Not Fit One Universal Ladder

Realms can differ radically in:

- the behavior of space and distance;
- the operation of time;
- dimensional structure, where dimensional language is applicable;
- their physical and metaphysical rules;
- accessibility and methods of traversal;
- whether a sentient observer can perceive, describe, map, or conceptualize them.

There is no established universal ladder that neatly orders every realm by size, dimensionality, or power. The existence of infinitely many realms above and below a given realm does not automatically create one consistent hierarchy.

Some realms may be conceptually impossible for sentient beings to map or represent. This is more than a temporary lack of information: the realm may not fit the observer's available concepts.

Do not flatten these differences into a single dimension count or conventional spatial ranking.

## 4. Earth as a Dense Knot of Connected Realities

Earth is a dense knot of connected realities, not the center of all existence.

Earth's destruction could collapse many realities that depend on it. It does not follow that every realm in existence would be destroyed.

Dependency can be disproportionate: a vast or even infinite realm may depend on a tiny local anchor. The size of the dependent realm does not require its anchor to be equally vast. The destruction of one anchor also does not prove that unrelated structures must fail.

The specific dependency network, the nature of its anchors, and the consequences of severing each relationship remain to be documented from source material. Do not invent a universal rule for all dependencies.

## 5. Great Red and Ophis

Great Red and Ophis are powerful but **high-mid** in the broader cosmological picture, not supreme beings or the final ceiling of existence.

Their importance within a familiar setting should not be confused with proof that they are the highest possible forms of existence. Greater entities and modes of being exist beyond them.

This is a statement about their place in this author's cosmology, not a claim about every external canon or powerscaling system.

## 6. Primordial Beings

Some greater entities are closer to living forces of nature than to people. They need not possess recognizable human consciousness, human-like motives, or familiar personalities.

Primordial beings may exist beyond ordinary human assumptions about:

- psychology and biology;
- familiar evolutionary processes;
- conventional time and eternity;
- thought as human beings understand it;
- ordinary harm and vulnerability.

Do not assume all primordial beings share identical properties. These are broad contrasts with ordinary human categories, not a complete universal specification.

### Intelligence and communication

Intelligence is not exclusive to recognizable human-style minds. It may involve processing, learning, adaptation, or forms of organization that do not resemble human cognition.

An interaction between primordial beings might look to an observer like stones communicating. An observer may detect that an interaction occurred without understanding what it meant, what was exchanged, or whether it corresponds to ordinary conversation at all.

Do not automatically translate unfamiliar cognition into human dialogue, emotion, or motivation. Equally, do not conclude that an entity lacks intelligence merely because its intelligence is inaccessible to human understanding.

## 7. “Shizink” Barrier — Term and Mechanics Unverified

**Name status:** “Shizink” is a phonetic or uncertain spelling. Verify the spelling against the original conversation before treating it as a finalized term.

The phenomenon described in working context is this: a realm cannot contain a particular entity. Rather than the entity simply being trapped, damaged, or expelled in an ordinary way, the realm collapses into the entity or becomes part of it. The transition leads to a primordial existence.

The precise conditions, sequence, exceptions, and metaphysical mechanics are not established in this record. Do not add triggers, stages, costs, limitations, or universal rules without source material.
