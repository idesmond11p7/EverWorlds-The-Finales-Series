# EverWorld 6 — Raw Idea Dump 001

> Status: RAW IDEATION ONLY
>
> This is not a requirements document, architecture, specification, canon lock, or implementation plan. It preserves the current stream of ideas so they can evolve without being prematurely formalized.

## Core Experience

EverWorld 6 is envisioned as the most interactive roleplay experience the creator has ever wanted: written replies and slash commands, movement, interactive game-like interfaces, and eventually many different ways of directly interacting with the fictional world.

The immediate fascination is worldbuilding: a radically expanded, evolved version of the High School DxD world that keeps the recognizable surface identity while deeply retconning and rebuilding most underlying mechanics.

The desired feeling is extreme epic fantasy rather than a conventional RPG spreadsheet.

## World / Power-System Direction

The world is not limited to magic.

There should be many fundamentally different power systems, races, resources, techniques, abilities, technologies, dimensional phenomena, mythological systems, and supernatural architectures.

Power should have an ecology.

Different powers can have:
- different resources;
- different regeneration mechanisms;
- different acquisition methods;
- different limitations;
- different grades;
- different subtypes;
- different compatibility rules;
- different evolutionary/devolutionary paths;
- different interactions with environments and other powers.

There may be 100+ distinct power/resource pools.

A pool is not necessarily a universal mana bar. It is more like ammunition in a large game ecosystem: different ammunition types have different scarcity, retrieval, recovery, and use patterns.

Two different abilities may share the same pool, but they do not have to.

An ability may interact with multiple pools.

Skills, abilities, powers, techniques, arts, magical spells, jutsu, elemental powers, technology, dimensional abilities, and other mechanisms should be modular rather than forced into one universal system.

The mathematics/system logic exists underneath the fiction, but the player should primarily experience the world as a mysterious reality rather than as a visible spreadsheet.

The purpose of having many pools and classifications is discovery: users should have to learn the world, investigate its systems, experiment, research, and gradually understand how things actually work.

The world can contain rules that no current character, canon character, or even existing fictional authority knows.

Some truths are simply fundamental properties of reality waiting to be discovered.

## Demons

“Demon” should be a broad classification rather than a single species.

The common core is that demons fundamentally feed on negative energy.

Different demons can consume different kinds of negative energy, including energy produced by their own kind.

The type of negative energy a demon consumes can influence:
- constitution;
- psychological instincts;
- abstract needs;
- behavior;
- complexity;
- stability;
- immortality;
- overall capability.

Feeding does not necessarily restore every power pool. A demon may feed one specific pool or aspect of its existence rather than receiving generic universal energy.

Demonic existence can range from:
- virtually spiritual entities;
- possessing/parasitic entities;
- territorial horrors;
- entities that torment people in homes;
- folklore-type beings;
- false gods;
- godlike or primordial demons;
- other extremely complex forms.

Not every godlike entity is necessarily a demon. Some gods may be unrelated classifications or other branches of existence.

The demon classification should be capable of containing many mythological, folkloric, cultural, and invented forms without requiring all of them to be the same species.

## Devils / Noble Demons

Devils should not simply be synonymous with demons.

A devil is envisioned as a dominant subtype / higher-order demon, potentially called a noble demon.

Devils are not necessarily the strongest beings.

They are dominant within the overall demonological ecosystem, but “dominant” does not mean:
- most common;
- highest tier;
- strongest.

Their defining relationship is feeding on negative emotions/energy associated with sentient beings, with selfishness/greed being an important conceptual connection.

The devil's underworld is only one underworld system.

The larger world contains multiple underworld/hell/death-domain systems:
- the devil-associated underworld;
- an actual burning hell;
- Coyote / other cultural underworlds;
- other disconnected or conditionally connected systems.

Some underworlds may be disconnected.

Some may connect only under strict conditions.

Some may overlap or interact without being the same realm.

The underworld is therefore an ecosystem/cosmology rather than one universal location.

Primordial demons and other ancient entities may exist above or outside the familiar devil hierarchy and can potentially exceed even extremely powerful DxD-derived figures.

## Angels

Angels should also be a broad parent classification rather than one simple race.

Angels do not feed on positive emotions.

Instead, they inspire or increase the possibility of positive emotional states, particularly in humans.

Their presence can therefore alter the emotional ecology around them.

A strong positive angelic presence can make an environment less hospitable to demons/devils by reducing the negative emotional/energetic conditions those entities depend on.

Angels have:
- different hierarchies;
- different jobs;
- different physiologies;
- different energy pools;
- different abilities;
- different levels of embodiment.

Some angels may:
- be spirits without bodies;
- possess bodies;
- have wings;
- have radically different physiology;
- resemble animals such as horses;
- be incapable of speech;
- function as living weapons;
- perform specialized non-combat jobs.

Not every angel should be a fighter.

There can be personal/guardian angels, higher angels, archangels, and many other orders.

The angelic hierarchy should be vastly larger than what canon explicitly presents.

Some angels can potentially be artificially created.

Different angel categories may be different races/architectures rather than merely different power levels.

## Guardian Angels

Guardian angels are not necessarily connected directly to the Biblical God.

A human can have a guardian angel associated with guidance, luck, positive emotional development, or metaphysical influence.

Some guardian angels may not even understand the larger angelic hierarchy.

Their existence can be metaphysical and indirect rather than combat-oriented.

Guardian angels have their own variations and tiers.

A personal/guardian angel is not necessarily the same kind of being as an archangel and should not automatically be able to “fall” in the same way.

## Fallen Angels

A fallen angel is not simply any angel that becomes evil.

A particular level/order of angel must be capable of falling.

The fall represents a fundamental inversion of function/relationship.

A sufficiently high-order angel that falls may lose the ability to inspire positive emotional states and instead begin consuming negative energy from humans.

Because the being already possessed a powerful angelic architecture, the inversion can produce an entity that remains extremely powerful and may become stronger in certain contexts.

The fallen angel can have different immunities and weaknesses from ordinary angels.

The relationship is conceptually similar to a mirrored/reversed power architecture rather than simply “angel but evil.”

Fallen angels primarily feed on negative energy from humans, which distinguishes them from broader demons that can feed on negative energy from many sources.

This narrower food source makes fallen angels rarer than demons/devils despite their potentially high baseline power.

## Holy Power / Full Light

Holy power should not simply be “the natural energy produced by angels.”

It should be a much more specialized, weaponized, sophisticated mechanism specifically capable of interacting with demonic physiology, demonic metaphysics, and abstract supernatural states.

“Full Light” or comparable high-order light abilities should function more like specialized anti-demonic weaponry than generic holy magic.

Its underlying operation should eventually have an empirical/scientifically grounded explanation compatible with real-world phenomena where useful, potentially involving fundamental physical/quantum-level ideas, while retaining mystery and abstraction.

The goal is not sci-fi technobabble.

The goal is to make supernatural mechanics feel empirically coherent without destroying their mystery.

## Hierarchy

The familiar DxD tier system should be expanded significantly.

Longer hierarchies and many subtypes are preferred.

A proposed angelic hierarchy could contain something like 21–72+ orders/categories, using culturally/mysteriously significant numbers where appropriate.

These categories should NOT necessarily represent a simple power ladder.

A category can represent:
- physiology;
- function/job;
- architecture;
- embodiment;
- specialization;
- role;
- power;
- or combinations of these.

Some tiers may therefore be incomparable rather than simply stronger/weaker.

## Sentient Skills / Abilities / Weapons

Skills, abilities, magical powers, and weapons can become highly developed.

At sufficiently advanced levels, a skill/ability/weapon may possess:
- sentience;
- behavior;
- evolution;
- internal complexity;
- developmental history;
- independent characteristics.

A skill is therefore not necessarily just an executable function.

Power itself can become a participant in the world's ecology.

## Discovery / Knowledge

The player should NOT automatically know all of this.

The world is meant to be researched and discovered.

Characters may misunderstand their own powers.

Existing characters may have incomplete or incorrect models of reality.

Some fundamental truths may be unknown to every character.

Lore can be discovered through:
- experience;
- experimentation;
- observation;
- conversations;
- research;
- books;
- encounters;
- failures;
- exploration.

The player's understanding of the world should therefore evolve alongside their character.

## High School DxD Relationship

Recognizable DxD surface systems should remain where they preserve the identity of the setting, including major recognizable concepts such as Sacred Gears and Evil Pieces.

Most other mechanics can be deeply retconned and rebuilt.

The goal is not to erase the feeling of DxD.

The goal is to make the world feel like a much larger, deeper epic-fantasy reality that could plausibly contain the recognizable DxD surface.

## Core Intuition

The world should feel like it is bigger than what the player currently understands.

The player should sometimes encounter a phenomenon and realize:

“I don't even know what system this belongs to.”

The eventual interactive/VR-like appeal comes partly from this: the player is not merely reading a database of powers; they are inhabiting a world whose ontology extends beyond their current knowledge.

This is intentionally incomplete.

Do not convert this document into formal requirements or architecture during ideation.
