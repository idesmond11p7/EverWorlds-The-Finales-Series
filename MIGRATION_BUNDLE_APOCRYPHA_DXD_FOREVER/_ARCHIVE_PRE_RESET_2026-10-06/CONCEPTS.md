# Concepts Register

Living register of EverWorlds concepts. Concepts may evolve before becoming requirements.

## Confirmed Concepts

### Finale
A Finale is a deeply evolved version of an existing world. It preserves the original world's identity, defining traits, and foundational characteristics while evolving it extensively into something unrecognizably better.

**Core relationship:** Preservation + Evolution.

### Digital Twin
EverWorlds outputs are usually envisioned as highly detailed digital twins of a world, a person, or another subject, with an emphasis on psychological realism.

### LLM Problem Mitigation
EverWorlds is fundamentally concerned with addressing basic and fundamental LLM issues that interfere with high-quality roleplay. The system is intended to account for known LLM limitations, tendencies, and failure patterns.

### Professional-Tier Standard
The project is intended to reach a professional-tier level of quality and discipline.
