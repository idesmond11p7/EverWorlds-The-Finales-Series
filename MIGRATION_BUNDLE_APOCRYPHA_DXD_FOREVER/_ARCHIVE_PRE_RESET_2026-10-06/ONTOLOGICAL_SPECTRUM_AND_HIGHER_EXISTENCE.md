# Ontological Spectrum & Higher Existence

## Status

**STATE: AUTHOR-ACCEPTED CANON / ACTIVE WORLD ARCHITECTURE**

**Scope lock:** This document records the author's ontological-spectrum model as it applies to the current Kuoh/cosmology architecture. It is a structural model, not a universal claim that every realm uses identical rules.

---

## 1. Core Principle

Existence is not represented by one simple power ladder.

The setting distinguishes between:

- physical depth;
- supernatural methodology;
- mode of existence;
- ontological depth;
- accessibility;
- causal compatibility;
- conceptual status.

**Power, ontology and accessibility are not interchangeable.**

---

## 2. Broad Spectrum

The spectrum extends from the physical toward increasingly deep forms of existence:

**Physical → Subatomic → Atomic → Fundamental → Magic → Mystical → Astral → Psychic → Mental → Semi-Spiritual/Astral Propagation → Full Spirituality 1 → Full Spirituality 2 → Lower Conceptual → Higher Conceptual → Metaconceptual → Absolute Being**

There are many intermediate layers.

The sequence is not a simple “who wins a fight” ranking.

---

## 3. Physical

Ordinary material existence.

Includes conventional matter, bodies, environments, observable forces and ordinary physical causality.

This is the lowest end of the spectrum.

---

## 4. Subatomic / Atomic / Fundamental

These are distinct physical depths.

### Subatomic
Particle-scale structures and interactions.

### Atomic
Atomic and molecular organization.

### Fundamental
The deepest physical descriptions available to the setting's physics, including fundamental particles/fields and related structures.

**Fundamental physics remains physical.**

---

## 5. Magic

Magic is a distinct supernatural methodology.

It is not merely “advanced physics” and it is not synonymous with mysticism.

Magic can have:

- rules;
- resources;
- conditions;
- techniques;
- compatibility requirements;
- limitations;
- measurable physical interfaces.

A sufficiently advanced scientific civilization may be able to model some magical effects without thereby explaining magic's entire ontology.

---

## 6. Mystical

Mystical is a distinct terminology/category from magic.

The two are connected and can interact, but they are not interchangeable.

Mystical phenomena can operate through different principles, relationships and ontological assumptions from magical systems.

**Mystical ≠ higher-level magic.**

**Magic ≠ mystical physics.**

---

## 7. Astral

Astral existence is nonphysical or partially interfacing existence.

It can include:

- astral bodies;
- astral consciousness;
- astral environments;
- astral traversal;
- astral reflections of physical places;
- nonphysical entities;
- astral routes;
- astral ecosystems.

Some astral layers correspond closely to physical Kuoh.

Others do not.

---

## 8. Psychic and Mental

Psychic and mental existence are related but distinct.

### Psychic
Concerned with consciousness-linked interaction, perception, psychic influence, psychic environments and related structures.

### Mental
Concerned with cognition, thought structures, internal realities and mental domains.

Neither category automatically reduces to astral existence.

---

## 9. Semi-Spiritual / Astral Propagation

This is an important bridge layer.

A higher or nonphysical phenomenon can sometimes propagate downward through a compatible intermediary.

The intermediary permits:

- influence;
- information;
- energy/effect;
- manifestation;
- interaction;

across an otherwise incompatible ontological boundary.

This is not simply a frequency or vibration issue.

It is an **ontological bridge**.

---

## 10. Full Spirituality 1 — Metaphysical / Abstract

This is a genuinely nonphysical mode of existence.

Its defining rule:

> **The physical spectrum cannot directly access it.**

This is not caused by insufficient technology.

It is not:

- a frequency problem;
- a vibration problem;
- a distance problem;
- a conventional dimensional-coordinate problem;
- an energy problem.

It is an **existential roadblock**.

The physical spectrum fundamentally does not possess the mode of existence required to directly access this layer.

Therefore Full Spirituality 1 cannot ordinarily physically interact with physical things.

A compatible semi-spiritual bridge can permit indirect interaction.

---

## 11. Full Spirituality 2 — Higher-Dimensional

Higher-Dimensional Spirituality is distinct from the metaphysical/abstract variant.

It should not automatically be treated as “Spirituality 1 but stronger.”

Its defining feature is a different relationship with dimensional/higher-space structures.

The metaphysical/abstract layer has the absolute physical inaccessibility rule described above.

Higher-Dimensional spirituality can have different forms of accessibility and interaction depending on its specific ontology.

---

## 12. Lower Conceptual

Conceptual existence begins becoming ontologically significant here.

Human-level examples include concepts such as:

- justice;
- right;
- wrong;
- fairness;
- duty;
- identity.

A human psychological concept does not automatically become an absolute cosmic entity.

**Conceptuality has levels.**

---

## 13. Higher Conceptual

Higher conceptual existence concerns concepts operating at deeper ontological levels than ordinary human cognition.

These concepts can possess existence or causal relevance beyond individual human thought.

They are not automatically equivalent to Metaconceptual existence.

---

## 14. Metaconceptual

Metaconceptual existence concerns structures that operate beyond ordinary concepts themselves.

The exact mechanics are intentionally not reduced to ordinary human language.

This is a high-end ontological category.

---

## 15. Absolute Being

Absolute Being represents the highest end of the current spectrum.

This is not merely:

> “the strongest character.”

It represents an ontological condition at the extreme limit of what existence permits.

---

## 16. Higher-Tier Scientific Boundary

The setting permits scientific explanations for supernatural phenomena where the phenomenon remains compatible with scientific modeling.

A Devil ability, Work Make, supernatural organism or traversal method may therefore have a strong scientific explanation for its observable behavior.

However, sufficiently high-tier entities can exceed what physics can meaningfully model.

Physics may be able to measure:

- effects;
- energy;
- particle behavior;
- environmental consequences;
- causal signatures;
- interaction patterns.

But it may fail to provide a physical origin for the entity's underlying power.

The model can effectively treat the deeper power as a **constant/input**.

This does not necessarily mean physics has been “broken.”

It means the physical model has reached the boundary of its explanatory domain.

---

## 17. Physics as Interface, Not Final Ontology

A supernatural phenomenon can therefore have:

**observable physical interface**

while possessing:

**nonphysical underlying ontology.**

A civilization can engineer around the interface without possessing a complete explanation of the source.

This allows:

- supernatural engineering;
- occult science;
- magical research;
- astral cartography;
- mystical scholarship;
- metaphysical philosophy;

to coexist.

Different civilizations may describe the same phenomenon using different explanatory languages.

---

## 18. Mythology + Physics Rule

The setting intentionally combines mythological worldbuilding with physics-informed mechanics.

Scientific terminology does not make the world science fiction.

A mechanism can have a technical description while remaining embedded in:

- myth;
- religion;
- supernatural politics;
- spiritual ecology;
- metaphysics;
- ancient civilizations;
- divine/occult traditions.

The world can therefore contain both:

**particle-stream transversion**

and

**mythic water-world traversal infrastructure**

without contradiction.

---

## 19. Ontology / Power / Accessibility

Always distinguish:

### Power
How much an entity can affect.

### Ontology
What kind of existence the entity possesses.

### Accessibility
What kinds of existence the entity can directly interact with.

### Causal Interface
What bridge or mechanism allows interaction across ontological boundaries.

A higher ontological entity does not automatically have unrestricted physical interaction.

A lower entity may possess extraordinary influence within a compatible layer.

---

## 20. Canon Boundary

This document defines the current **ontological-spectrum framework**.

It does not automatically define:

- every Work Make;
- every Devil ability;
- every species' exact ontological tier;
- every objective realm's placement;
- every metaphysical entity;
- the complete mechanics of Absolute Being.

Those require separate canonical decisions.

---

## 21. One-Sentence Canon

> **The setting contains a deep spectrum of existence extending from ordinary physical reality through increasingly nonphysical, spiritual, conceptual, metaconceptual and ultimately absolute modes of being; magic and mysticism remain distinct but connected categories, and at sufficiently high tiers physics can model effects while treating the underlying power as an unexplained constant because the source exceeds the physical model's ontological domain.**
