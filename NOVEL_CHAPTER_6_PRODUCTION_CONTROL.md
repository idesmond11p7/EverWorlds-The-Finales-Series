# Production Architecture

## Beat 1 — Apartment: inhabitation

**Function:** Make the apartment feel used rather than merely assigned.

Required material:
- morning light;
- objects Jonah/Ira has already handled;
- clothes;
- exam papers;
- money;
- school materials;
- breakfast preparation;
- ordinary domestic friction;
- subtle body familiarity.

Do not repeat the previous chapter's identity discovery.

### Internal movement
He does not suddenly think “this is my home.” Instead, he behaves as though it is becoming one: knowing where something is, using an object without hesitation, checking a drawer, preparing food, then noticing how natural the action felt.

### Verification
No invented Ira backstory. Objects may reveal present facts only.

---

## Beat 2 — Preparation

**Function:** Establish practical competence.

He checks:
- documents;
- money;
- route;
- school materials;
- time.

The route must be a physical problem, not a sentence saying “I checked the route.”

The period should emerge through paper, station information, ordinary phones/technology, printed material, and physical navigation rather than exposition.

### Verification
No smartphone navigation, modern app behaviour, or QR-code culture.

## Beat 8 — Getting lost / recovery

- asking a staff member or passenger;

The recovery should make him more self-reliant.

# Scene Density Rule

The chapter target is **3,000–4,000 words**, but length is earned through lived movement.

No travel-summary paragraphs whose only job is to move Jonah between locations.

Every major location needs:
1. physical position;
2. immediate surroundings;
3. movement;
4. sound;
5. light/material interaction;
6. Jonah's actual attention;
7. an action that changes his position or knowledge.

Not every paragraph needs all seven.

# Prose Control

### Required
- flowing first-person;
- varied sentence length;
- substantial paragraphs;
- concrete physical description;
- natural dialogue;
- humour from Jonah's reactions;
- scene-specific rhythm;
- occasional short sentences only when they earn their place.

### Prohibited drift
- fragment chains;
- fake cinematic cadence;
- “AI trailer” prose;
- generic beauty declarations;

- meta jokes about being in a story;

# Research Locks

## Period transit

The period is 2005/2006.

JR East documentation establishes that Suica was already widely deployed by 2005, with stored-fare cards and automated gates; Mobile Suica began in January 2006. Therefore a paper ticket / physical ticket-machine interaction remains a safe narrative choice, while modern smartphone navigation is not.

Source:
- https://www.jreast.co.jp/e/investor/ar/2005/pdf/ar2005_all.pdf
- https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/e/press/20051101/index.html

## Geography

Do **not** invent an exact real-world apartment address or claim a precise canonical real-world location for Kuoh. The story can establish a plausible local neighbourhood → rail journey → Kuoh approach without pretending an unsupported map coordinate is canon.

## School administration

Use ordinary administrative behaviour: documents, queues, staff directions, waiting areas, applicant movement. Do not invent specific examination rules unless Chapter 7 research establishes them.

# Draft Verification Gates

Before calling the prose complete:

### Gate A — Continuity
- Does Chapter 6 begin after Chapter 5's established state?
- Is the exam already known to be today?
- Is Ira's identity already known?
- Is Kuoh already known?
- Is there no repeated identity-discovery scene?

### Gate B — Knowledge
- Does Jonah only perceive what he could know?
- Is no supernatural knowledge leaked through POV?
- Does Sona remain unexplained?

### Gate C — Journey
- Does the reader feel elapsed travel time?
- Are there multiple physical transitions?
- Is the journey spatially coherent?
- Is the mistake believable and different from Chapter 5?

### Gate D — Habitation
- Does the apartment feel inhabited?
- Does food exist as actual experience?
- Are ordinary objects doing narrative work?
- Does Jonah behave more naturally in the body?

### Gate E — World rendering
- Can the reader mentally place Jonah in each major location?
- Are light, surfaces, sound, movement, and distance physically coherent?
- Does Kuoh emerge from details rather than exposition?

### Gate F — Prose
- Are fragments controlled?
- Are paragraphs allowed to breathe?
- Is humour character-derived rather than meta?
- Is the chapter visually rich without adjective inflation?

### Gate G — Boundary
- Does the chapter stop at 9:00 AM?
- Does Chapter 7 still own the examination?
