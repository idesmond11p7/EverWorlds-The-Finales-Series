## Analogy requirement for future energy documentation

Every analogy used to explain an energy mechanic must be included in the actual relevant document. Record:

1. **Mechanic:** the literal fictional rule.
2. **Analogy:** the concrete comparison that makes the rule understandable.
3. **Mapping:** which parts of the analogy correspond to which parts of the mechanic.
4. **Boundary:** where the comparison stops being accurate.
5. **Example:** at least one consequence or scenario demonstrating the mechanic.
6. **Status/source:** canon, author proposal, historical idea, assistant proposal, or unresolved.

Never write an analogy that silently introduces a new mechanic. Never let a helpful analogy replace exact terminology or erase exceptions.
