# Research and Recovery Method

STATUS: ACTIVE — RECOVERY METHODOLOGY
LAST VERIFIED: 2026-10-06

## Purpose
Recover durable project knowledge without laundering reconstruction into canon.

## Method
### 1. Inventory
List every archived file and classify its apparent function.

### 2. Read
Read the full source, not just filenames or headings.

### 3. Extract
Separate exact claims, quotations, decisions, chronology, character knowledge, mechanics, research evidence, and proposals.

### 4. Cross-check
Compare claims against other archived sources and Git history. Record contradictions instead of averaging them.

### 5. Research
Where the archive contains a factual claim requiring external verification, research credible primary/authoritative sources where practical. Record the source and date.

### 6. Promote
Only information with traceable provenance becomes active documentation.

### 7. Quarantine
Reconstructed prose, obsolete plans, speculative concepts, and contradicted claims remain preserved but cannot silently feed new generation.

### 8. Link
Every recovered fact points to its source and affected chapters/documents.

## Research boundary
External research can verify real-world facts and source-setting facts. It cannot override an explicit author decision about the project's fictional canon.

## Evidence labels
CONFIRMED / VERIFIED / DERIVED / PROVISIONAL / UNKNOWN / CONFLICTED / RETRACTED.

## No false precision
Unknown dates, times, quotes, and source identities remain UNKNOWN. Never fill missing metadata because a complete-looking record feels cleaner.
