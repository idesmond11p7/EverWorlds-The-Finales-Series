# 08 — Canon Event Anchors and Manga Continuity Register (Pass 1)

**Status:** Partial research register. This is not a full chapter-by-chapter synopsis. It separates official publisher-confirmed framing from secondary-reference leads that still require manga-panel verification.

## 1. Primary-source priority

The main manga consists of 15 collected volumes and ends at Chapter 78. Yen Press's official Volume 15 listing identifies the final volume as the decisive battle for the Empire and lists the contents as Chapters 73–78. Its synopsis names the Emperor's Supreme Teigu, Tatsumi, Esdeath, Akame, Night Raid and the Revolutionary Army's final Teigu-wielding regiment as central to the ending.

Official source:
- Yen Press, *Akame ga KILL!, Vol. 15*: https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975300449-akame-ga-kill-vol-15
- Yen Press series page: https://yenpress.com/series/akame-ga-kill?format=Digital

This official synopsis confirms the final-volume framing, not every event detail. Character-level outcomes below are secondary-reference leads until checked directly against Chapter 78 and relevant earlier chapters.

## 2. High-priority ending-state leads (Chapter 78)

The final chapter must be transcribed from the manga before the RPG locks its ending-state table. Secondary chapter summaries currently indicate these points for verification:

- The Revolutionary Army reaches the palace and the final confrontation with Minister Honest concludes.
- The Emperor is executed after the regime's collapse.
- Leone's final condition and death are distinct from the anime's version.
- Tatsumi survives in the Tyrant's body; Mine wakes from her coma, and the ending indicates their future together and a child.
- Akame departs by sea to seek a cure for her condition and a way to restore Tatsumi.
- Suzuka survives and leaves the continent.
- The northern tribes' fate, postwar relations with western peoples, and the dismantling/reorganization of forces are addressed in the epilogue.
- Night Raid does not receive a simple public heroic legacy; the history remembered by later generations is politically complicated.

**Secondary lead:** https://akamegakill.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_78  
**Caution:** Do not copy this summary as the canon record. Verify panel by panel against the licensed manga. If a detail conflicts with the actual manga, the manga wins.

## 3. Manga/anime divergence policy

A secondary adaptation guide identifies substantial divergence by approximately anime Episode 17 and recommends resuming the manga around Volume 7, Chapter 33 if someone has only watched the anime. That is a reader convenience estimate, not a substitute for comparing both versions.

Reference: https://wheredoestheanimeleaveoff.com/where-does-the-akame-ga-kill-anime-leave-off-in-the-manga/

Rules:
- Never use the anime-original final events as the RPG's default manga continuity.
- Do not infer that an event is identical merely because its broad premise appears in both adaptations.
- Maintain a divergence table with fields: manga chapter, anime episode, manga event, anime event, difference type, downstream continuity impact, source confidence.
- The manga's Wild Hunt arc and later events must be included in the chronology, not silently skipped because the anime handled them differently.
- A character's survival, injury, allegiance, knowledge or location must use the manga version at the selected timeline point.

## 5. Geography facts: source confidence discipline

Secondary geography indexes describe a world with multiple countries, diverse climate zones, mountains, deserts, tundra, islands and Danger Beast habitats. They also describe a Great Canal, Putra's arid canyon environment and waterworks, and the Wakoku setting.

These are research leads, not permission to freeze every number or claim as canon. For every geographic feature, record:
- manga volume/chapter and page/panel if available;
- exact Japanese or licensed translated name;
- depiction and directional clues;
- relation to other named locations;
- travel or climate clues;
- whether a map is shown;
- confidence and contradictions;
- whether the entry is directly depicted, stated in dialogue, stated in supplementary material or inferred.

Secondary index:
- https://akamegakill.fandom.com/wiki/Geography

The frequently repeated Great Canal length and construction-duration claims must remain **unverified** until a primary-source passage is located. Same for exact political boundaries and comprehensive world-map claims.

## 6. Canon vs expansion labels

Use these labels in every downstream document:
- **MANGA-CONFIRMED:** directly supported by the main manga.
- **SUPPLEMENT-CONFIRMED:** supported by official or author-approved supplementary material, with source named.
- **SECONDARY-LEAD:** reported by a secondary source; not yet directly verified.
- **SUPPORTED-INFERENCE:** best explanation of source evidence, but not explicitly stated.
- **ORIGINAL-EXPANSION:** authored for this RPG.
- **GENERATED-LOCAL:** generated under approved world rules and saved as persistent world data.
- **UNKNOWN / CONFLICTED:** no reliable basis or contradictory sources.

Never promote an entry's status merely because it has been repeated in multiple fan pages. Record evidence quality, not the number of copies.
