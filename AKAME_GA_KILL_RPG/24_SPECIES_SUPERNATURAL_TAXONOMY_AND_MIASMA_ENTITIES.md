# 24. Species and Supernatural Taxonomy: Miasma, Void, and Non-Danger-Beast Entities
Status: classification framework and original expansion. Individual entities are not manga canon unless separately verified.

## Taxonomy rule
Danger Beast is a setting-specific category, not a universal label for every unusual organism. Distinguish ordinary fauna, Danger Beasts, sapient peoples, spirits, constructed entities, anomalous organisms, extradimensional entities and environmental phenomena. Appearance alone does not determine biology, morality, intelligence or powers.

## Registry fields
Every recurring type needs a stable ID; names used by different cultures; classification and confidence; body plan, senses, locomotion, respiration, nutrition and reproduction/spawn mechanism where known; habitat, range, climate and ecological dependencies; intelligence and communication evidence; threat and defensive behavior; capabilities, costs, vulnerabilities and hard limits; relationships with other species; resource value and harvesting rules; contamination effects; population cycle and recovery time; and source status.

## Categories
1. Ordinary fauna: familiar ecological needs, with specific fantasy adaptations only where defined.
2. Danger Beasts: canon-associated category; individual species can have unusual traits and may be harvested. Not every dangerous animal belongs here.
3. Sapient peoples: cultures and political communities, not monster loot tables. Biology does not imply shared morality or government.
4. Spirits and incorporeal entities: persistence may depend on place, ritual, memory or explicit conditions; not all are ghosts.
5. Constructed entities: artificial organisms, ritual constructs, alchemical creations or engineered guardians; makers and maintenance matter.
6. Anomalous organisms: traits outside known biology but with observed patterns, limits and habitats.
7. Extradimensional entities: crossing requires a defined mechanism and may be rare, costly or hazardous.
8. Environmental phenomena: miasma fields, void pockets, spatial folds or living weather that may not be individual creatures.
9. Unresolved cases: classification remains open until evidence exists. Uncertainty is not permission for arbitrary powers.

## Purple-void and miasma phenomena
Purple void is a visual direction, not a universal element or one species. Several unrelated phenomena use this aesthetic but have different mechanisms.

### SV-001 — Violet Miasma Bloom
A vapor anomaly around certain mineral seams, decaying matter or unstable underground flows. Density and light shift its color from bruised violet to near-black. It distorts contrast and can impair balance and orientation; it does not automatically dissolve matter or control minds. It pools in low, poorly ventilated spaces and moves with wind and thermal gradients. Risks include respiratory injury and vertigo. Ventilation, exposure limits, sealed equipment and measured retreat routes reduce danger. Some organisms avoid it; others use its chemical gradients as habitat cues.

### SV-002 — The Null Veil
A spatial anomaly that disrupts selected sensory signals in a bounded volume. Echoes terminate early and moving objects seem to lose depth. It is not ordinary darkness or a universal anti-magic field. It does not automatically stop projectiles or erase matter. Boundaries are irregular; instruments become unreliable and crossing can cause disorientation.

### SV-003 — Gloam Drifters
Entities sometimes observed along boundaries between ordinary air and certain miasma blooms. Their silhouettes look jointed at a distance, but close observations disagree. They approach heat, retreat from abrupt pressure changes and sometimes follow airflow. Their intelligence and ecological role are unresolved. They may be hostile, defensive, curious or indifferent; do not assume one behavior for every encounter. They are not infinite-spawn enemies.

### SV-004 — The Deep Hush
An acoustic anomaly in selected caverns where sound is absorbed or redirected. Voices may be audible in one direction but not the reverse. It does not erase visual evidence or grant telepathy. Underground communities develop tactile signals and physical route markers to cope with it.

### SV-005 — Ashen Aperture
A rare localized spatial tear whose interior appears violet-black and starless. Triggers remain unknown; reports associate appearances with geological violence or failed high-energy rituals, but causation is unproven. Geometry nearby becomes difficult to measure and objects may be displaced or damaged. It has no reliable destination, no guaranteed return and no repeatable activation procedure. Never use it as convenient fast travel.

### SV-006 — Miasma-Born Witnesses
A disputed category of entities able to persist in certain toxic or anomalous vapors. Some may be organisms; others may be optical appearances through distorted media. They exchange signals through scent, condensate, pressure and motion. Signal interpretation is uncertain, and the category may contain multiple unrelated kinds.

### SV-007 — Hollow Lichen
A colony organism on abandoned stone in regions with low-level spatial anomalies. It produces thin purple filaments and hollow nodules that resonate as air pressure changes. Uses include pigments and acoustic markers. Spores irritate lungs and overharvesting destabilizes colonies. It does not store souls or record perfect memories.

## Spawn and population rules
Every spawn factor must name a mechanism: ordinary reproduction, budding/colony division, seasonal emergence from dormancy, migration, construction by a maker, manifestation around a persistent environmental condition, or a rare anomaly with no repeatable cause. Each mechanism has different rates, resource needs, delays and population caps. Do not spawn enemies solely because the player enters a room. Populations change through food, habitat, mortality, migration, hunting and reproduction delays.

## Encounter rules
- Establish evidence that lets the character detect the entity.
- Respect range, line of sight, acoustics, visibility, wind and cover.
- Do not reveal internal classifications without a plausible information source.
- Unknown entities act according to observed patterns, not whatever is most dramatic.
- A creature may flee, hide, defend territory, investigate, feed, sleep, migrate or ignore the player.
- Defeat does not guarantee loot. Harvesting requires knowledge, tools, time and safe handling.
- Rare entities remain rare. Do not flood the world with purple anomalies because the aesthetic is appealing.

## Safeguards
Never infer intelligence from humanoid shape, morality from species, shared origin from shared color, or supernatural ability from a name. Keep body, behavior, culture and metaphysical classification separate.
