# EverWorlds — Supernatural Reality / Magic Architecture

## Status

**State:** ACTIVE CONCEPTUAL CANON / EXPANDING SYSTEM

This document records the current author-defined framework for magic, reality manipulation, Work Makes, Aimor, and the distinction between ordinary magic and higher metaphysical interaction.

It does **not** claim that real-world quantum physics proves the fictional system. Real physics is used as conceptual inspiration and vocabulary only.

---

## 1. Magic and Reality Manipulation

**Magic is fundamentally reality manipulation within the classical/quantum-limited supernatural layer of EverWorlds.**

Magic is not a separate substance that sits outside reality. A magical effect is an intervention into reality that produces an outcome ordinary classical/Newtonian physics cannot naturally produce.

For current setting purposes:

> **Magic = reality manipulation at the magic-layer scale.**

This does **not** automatically mean:

- conceptual manipulation;
- metaphysical concept rewriting;
- timeline manipulation;
- spacetime manipulation;
- multiversal manipulation;
- unrestricted causality manipulation.

Those are distinct capabilities that require their own basis.

### Quantum boundary

The setting treats magic as operating at a level associated with phenomena beyond ordinary classical mechanics and uses quantum mechanics as a conceptual substrate for its mystery.

The Many-Worlds interpretation may exist as an interpretive analogy within the setting, but it is **not** a blanket rule that magic creates universes, changes timelines, or manipulates four-dimensional spacetime.

The purpose is to preserve mystery rather than reduce every supernatural event to an in-world physics explanation.

**Important:** Real quantum mechanics does not establish this fictional hierarchy. The fictional system borrows the conceptual gap between classical intuition and deeper physical description.

---

## 2. Higher Metaphysical Reality

There are entities and states of existence that operate at a level **deeper than ordinary magic**.

These higher entities should not automatically be described as merely using stronger magic.

Their interaction with reality can involve a more fundamental metaphysical layer.

Therefore:

**Magic layer**
→ reality manipulation within the established supernatural/physical framework

**Higher metaphysical layer**
→ interaction with structures deeper than ordinary magic

The exact higher-layer mechanics remain to be recovered/defined rather than being invented casually.

---

## 3. Reality Work / Work Make

The supernatural system uses **Work Makes** as structured reality-interaction functions.

A Work Make is not simply a spell name.

It is a constructed supernatural function that produces a defined effect through interaction with reality.

A Work Make can therefore specify or embody:

- function;
- target;
- conditions;
- range;
- manifestation;
- resource compatibility;
- limitations;
- failure conditions;
- interaction rules.

The same underlying reality can therefore support radically different supernatural architectures.

---

## 4. Demonic Reality Work

**Demonic Reality Work** is reality manipulation expressed through demonic architecture.

The term "demonic" describes the **method and energy architecture**, not an absolute statement that only the biological/species category called demons can ever interact with reality this way.

The core demonic principle is:

> **Absorb supernatural energy → process/store it → reproduce an effect or outcome through reality interaction.**

This is why demonic reality work can appear unusually self-authored or flexible.

Devils are therefore not simply "magic users with stronger spells." Their architecture allows them to construct effects from absorbed resources and reproduce desired outcomes through their demonic reality-work system.

---

## 5. Aimor Pools

**Aimor** is the usable supernatural resource framework.

An **Aimor pool** is a specific reservoir/category of usable Aimor available to an entity or system.

An Aimor pool is not necessarily equivalent to a generic mana bar.

Different pools can have different:

- qualities;
- compatibilities;
- functions;
- conversion behavior;
- Work Make affinities;
- limits.

Therefore two entities can possess large supernatural reserves while having very different practical ability architectures.

---

## 6. Demons, Devils, and Fallen Angels

These entities all ultimately interact with reality, but their **energy ecology, instincts, and Work Make architecture** differ.

### Devils

Core tendency:

> **Termination / consumption of potential / apocalyptic finality.**

Devils are **Enders**. Their deeper ecological orientation is toward bringing active potential and trajectories to an end.

Devils feed on **SIN-associated negative energy and negative emotional energy**. **Negative potential is not a replacement fuel** and is not synonymous with misery. It is the **polar opposite of positive potential** and forms part of the deeper potential ecology that explains why Devil predation is especially interested in people with meaningful positive trajectories.

The important distinction is:

- **negative energy / SIN** = part of the Devil's feeding ecology;
- **negative potential** = the opposite pole of positive potential;
- **Ender instinct** = termination/finality and the destruction or consumption of potential.

A person who is already permanently miserable or effectively ended is not automatically an ideal target. If there is little meaningful positive trajectory left to terminate, a Devil may move on and seek someone whose life is still rising.

A person becoming confident, successful, connected, ambitious, spiritually stronger, or otherwise developing substantial positive potential can therefore be a more meaningful target. The relevant attraction is to **rise and the potential that can subsequently be ended**, not simply to a static quantity of suffering.

See `DEVIL_DEMON_NEGATIVE_POTENTIAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` for the authoritative detailed relationship.

Relevant conceptual vocabulary:

- nether;
- terminal;
- apocalyptic;
- catastrophic;
- cataclysmic;
- devastating;
- annihilative;
- extinction;
- ruin;
- finality.

"Nether" is a conceptual category, not a required visual/color code.

### Fallen Angels

Core tendency:

> **Continuation / torment / anguish / prolongation of negative states.**

Fallen angels tend toward preserving and extending negative trajectories rather than fundamentally terminating them.

Relevant conceptual vocabulary:

- anguish;
- torment;
- corruption;
- repetition;
- persistence;
- captivity;
- degradation;
- prolongation;
- despair;
- continuation.

This does **not** mean fallen angels cannot kill or terminate something.

Individual psychology, orders, strategy, and circumstance can override or redirect species-level instinct.

Likewise, devils can torture or prolong suffering if their individual motives or circumstances lead them to do so.

The distinction is therefore one of **instinctive/ontological imprint**, not an absolute behavioral prohibition.

### Angelic / Guardian Existence

Core tendency:

> **Continuation and reinforcement of positive potential.**

A guardian-type entity can guide, protect, reinforce favorable trajectories, increase favorable outcomes, improve luck, stabilize a person, or otherwise prolong beneficial development.

"Guardian angel" does not necessarily mean a literal Biblical-style angel.

It can refer to a supernatural existence, construct, or formation associated with guidance and reinforcement.

## 7. Supernatural Imprint

Different entities can perform overlapping actions while leaving different supernatural signatures.

### Fallen angel imprint

Often feels like:

> **This suffering is going to continue.**

Evidence may show repetition, dependency, corruption, recurring intrusion, prolonged degradation, or a structured cycle.

### Devil imprint

Often feels like:

> **Something has been ended.**

The horror comes from finality, catastrophic compression, irreversible loss, and the destruction of potential.

A devil can still torture, mutilate, manipulate, or prolong suffering. Those behaviors do not erase its underlying termination-oriented imprint.

The distinction is not:

**fallen angel = torture**

**devil = instant death**

The distinction is:

**fallen angel = continuation-oriented instinct**

**devil = termination-oriented instinct**

---

## 8. Instinct vs Psychology

Species-level supernatural instincts do not completely determine individual psychology.

The framework is analogous to biological instinct:

> **Ontology supplies pressure. Psychology interprets it. Agency chooses behavior.**

A supernatural entity can possess an instinct toward a particular mode of interaction while deliberately acting against that instinct.

Therefore a fallen angel may terminate something immediately.

A devil may preserve something.

Neither exception invalidates the underlying architecture.

---

## 9. Power Construction Principle

The setting should not treat supernatural abilities as arbitrary lists of powers.

A character's capabilities should be derived through:

**Ontology → energy architecture → Aimor pools → Work Make architecture → ability classes → individual specialization.**

This is the current design principle for constructing characters such as Raynare.

---

## 10. Color Is Not Ontology

Purple, red, black, gold, etc. are **not** canonical energy colors for these categories.

Color may be used as private visualization shorthand during design, but it must not become a mechanical classification.

A devil does not have to look red.

A fallen angel does not have to look purple.

Their distinction should emerge from:

- behavior;
- sensory impression;
- supernatural imprint;
- energy interaction;
- Work Make behavior;
- ecological function;
- consequences.

Raynare should be built as a fallen-angel reality-work specialist rather than as a generic "magic user."

Her eventual capability architecture should be developed through classes such as:

- sensory perception;

- elemental interaction;
