# EverWorlds Research Note — Prose as Perceptual Rendering

## Purpose
Support the novel's grounded visual/world-rendering standard with research rather than intuition.

## Findings

### 1. Readers construct situation models
Narrative comprehension research describes integrated mental representations of a described state of affairs (“situation models”), including spatial, temporal, causal, character, goal, and object information. citeturn0search4turn0search7

### 2. Spatial position is tracked
Experiments show that readers update spatial information relative to a protagonist's location and that accessibility changes with spatial separation. This supports writing environments as actual spatial systems rather than lists of visual adjectives. citeturn0search0turn0search5

### 3. Inference matters
Readers can incorporate spatial relations that are implied rather than explicitly stated. Therefore physical consequences can communicate environment without explicitly naming every property. citeturn0search0turn0search1

### 4. Attention changes event updating
Readers' attention to character and spatial dimensions affects when situation models are updated. This supports using paragraph/sentence boundaries deliberately when the viewpoint's attention changes. citeturn0search10

### 5. Grounded game-world reference
Red Dead Redemption's development emphasized researched environments, NPC schedules, distinct behaviours, and ecological rules because static or arbitrary world content could feel fake. That is useful as a design analogy for EverWorlds' prose: the world should behave independently of the protagonist. citeturn0search45

## Application

The prose rendering stack is:

**space → light → material → atmosphere → motion → sound → colour → attention → interpretation → cadence**

Do not use “beautiful,” “dark,” “cheerful,” or “cinematic” as substitutes for these physical inputs.

The reader should infer the visual/emotional result from the rendered conditions.

## Important Separation

**Lighting is not mood.**

A scene may be:
- bright + sad;
- dark + safe;
- rainy + peaceful;
- sunny + threatening.

**Beauty is not brightness.**

Naturalistic colour can be rich without being saturated.

**Realism is not darkness.**

Grounded realism comes from coherent physical relationships, spatial continuity, material behaviour, independent activity, and perspective.

## Production Consequence

Chapter 6 is the first deliberate application of this standard.

The goal is not to “showcase” Kuoh.

The goal is to make the reader's internal model of Kuoh increasingly stable and spatially coherent while Ira physically travels through it.
