# EverWorlds Chapter 6 — Production Blueprint

## Status
**Active production target**

## Function
Make Jonah/Ira's new life feel **lived before it becomes extraordinary**.

Chapter 5 establishes that Jonah has found somewhere to go and that the entrance examination is imminent. The accepted later chronology places the examination on the subsequent Chapter 6 day.

Chapter 6 answers the next question:
> **What does that “somewhere” actually feel like when he has to live inside it?**

## Structural Rule

Chapter 6 is **not**:
- a lore dump;
- a supernatural introduction;
- an entrance-exam chapter;
- a power reveal;
- a travel montage;
- a showcase of every interesting DxD character.

It is the habitation chapter.

## Sequence

1. **Apartment / morning anchor**
   - Ira wakes in the apartment.
   - Establishes what is now physically familiar.
   - Prepares for the examination.
   - Uses ordinary objects and practical logistics.
   - The apartment becomes the first place he can meaningfully call “mine,” even if he cannot yet emotionally claim it.

2. **Leaving**
   - Locks the apartment.
   - Navigates the immediate neighbourhood.
   - Ordinary residents, shops, utilities, traffic, pedestrians.
   - No supernatural explanation.

3. **Station**
   - Ticketing.
   - Platform.
   - Waiting.
   - Other passengers.
   - Announcements.
   - Physical crowd movement.

4. **Transit**
   - Long enough to establish distance.
   - Multiple visual/environmental transitions.
   - Ira notices how the city changes.
   - Period-appropriate technology/behaviour.
   - Food or drink should enter naturally rather than as a checklist.

5. **Stops / transfers**
   - Give the journey spatial memory.
   - Ira should know roughly where he has been by the time he reaches Kuoh.
   - Do not teleport between locations through prose.

6. **Getting lost / recovery**
   - A mundane mistake.
   - Interaction with ordinary people.
   - Small act of competence.
   - The mistake should teach the reader something about the city.

7. **Kuoh approach**
   - Environmental identity emerges through accumulated details.
   - Do not announce “Kuoh is special.”
   - Let architecture, people, school traffic, vegetation, commerce, and atmosphere establish it.

8. **Academy**
   - Registration/arrival.
   - School grounds and student population.
   - Social hierarchy visible through behaviour, not exposition.
   - Ira remains an outsider.

9. **Brief unexplained Sona appearance**
   - Sona is seen, not introduced through lore.
   - Ira does not know who she is.
   - The moment should register as a person/event, not a character wiki entry.
   - No Rias appearance.
   - No supernatural explanation.

10. **9:00 AM bell**
   - The bell begins the entrance examination.
   - Cut Chapter 6 there.

## Information Revealed

By the end:
- Ira knows how to physically reach the Academy.
- Ira has experienced Kuoh as a real place rather than a name on paper.
- Ira has begun building a practical routine.
- Reader understands the geography through lived movement.
- Reader has a clearer sense of ordinary society.
- Reader may notice unusual beauty/physical distinctiveness without receiving an explanation.
- Sona can be visually established without being explained.

## Information Withheld

Do not reveal:
- devil hierarchy;
- Rias's position;
- Sona's position;
- supernatural factions;
- Sacred Gears;
- power systems;
- Work Makes;
- spiritual food chain;
- Church exception;
- supernatural reason Kuoh is important;
- Ira's complete backstory;
- the nature of Ira's power.

## Prose Mode

Chapter 6 uses:
- grounded first-person;
- flowing cadence;
- concrete physical detail;
- natural dialogue;
- environmental continuity;
- humour emerging from Ira;
- occasional visual richness.

Avoid:
- constant fragments;
- horror cadence;
- exposition paragraphs;
- “cinematic” adjective piles;
- neon/over-saturated rendering;
- generic anime spectacle.

## World Rendering

Every major location should answer:
- What is the light doing?
- What materials are visible?
- What is moving independently?
- What sounds establish distance?
- What does Ira actually notice?
- What physical action changes his position?

The reader should construct Kuoh rather than receive a brochure about Kuoh.

## Character Movement

Ira begins:
**“I have somewhere to go.”**

He ends:
**“I have physically entered the place where my next life will begin.”**

The physical entry occurs on the new Chapter 6 day before the 9:00 AM examination bell.

That is the chapter's actual movement.

## Ending

The 9:00 AM bell rings.

The examination begins.

**Cut.**

Chapter 7 owns the exam.
