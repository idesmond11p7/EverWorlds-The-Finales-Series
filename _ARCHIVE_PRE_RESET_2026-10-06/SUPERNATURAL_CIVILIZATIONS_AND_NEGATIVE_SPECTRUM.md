# Supernatural Civilizations & Negative-Spectrum Ecology

## Status

**STATE: AUTHOR-ACCEPTED CANON / ACTIVE WORLD ARCHITECTURE**

**Scope:** This document establishes the broad civilization/ecology model for supernatural populations encountered in relation to Earth and Kuoh. It does **not** collapse all supernatural civilizations into Kuoh's local territorial system.

---

## 1. Core Principle

The supernatural world is not one civilization, one species hierarchy, or one unified political structure.

Human terminology often groups radically different entities under broad labels such as:

- demon;
- demonic entity;
- demonology;
- Nether;
- Abyss;
- Rift;
- Inferno;
- Hell;
- underworld;
- negative-spectrum entity.

These labels are often **classification language**, not proof that all entities sharing a label belong to the same civilization, species, realm, government, or ontology.

---

## 2. Earth-Facing Classification

When describing supernatural entities from a human/Earth perspective, broad categories may function as **entry-level classifications**.

For example, a human occultist may classify something as a:

> Demon.

That classification can be useful while remaining radically incomplete.

The entity may belong to:

- an enormous civilization;
- a hidden species;
- an extinct civilization's remnant;
- a unique plane;
- a specialized spiritual ecology;
- an independent negative-spectrum culture;
- a civilization unknown to most other supernatural powers.

Therefore:

**“Demon” does not mean “member of the Devil civilization.”**

And:

**Devils are one civilization/group among many supernatural populations associated with the negative spectrum.**

---

## 3. Negative Spectrum

The negative spectrum is a broad ecological/cosmological category rather than a single empire.

It can contain:

- Devils;
- demons;
- abyssal entities;
- infernal civilizations;
- Nether-associated populations;
- Rift-associated beings;
- entities associated with negative spiritual/ecological forces;
- extinct or fragmented civilizations;
- independent populations whose existence humans interpret through demonic terminology.

Different populations can have radically different:

- feeding mechanisms;
- ontologies;
- cultures;
- intelligence;
- politics;
- metaphysics;
- aesthetics;
- moral systems;
- physical forms;
- spiritual architectures;
- relationships with humans.

They should not be homogenized.

---

## 4. Scale

There can be **dozens to hundreds of civilizations, populations, factions, remnants and independent societies** associated with broad negative-spectrum classifications.

This is not a claim that every one is equally large or equally powerful.

The spectrum can contain:

- major civilizations;
- minor civilizations;
- isolated communities;
- nomadic populations;
- ancient remnants;
- extinct civilizations;
- hidden societies;
- territorial populations;
- parasitic ecologies;
- political confederations;
- fragmented survivors;
- entities with no conventional civilization at all.

---

## 5. Knowledge Is Fragmentary

Supernatural knowledge is not globally complete.

Some civilizations know of each other.

Some know only rumors.

Some have never encountered each other.

Some entities possess records of civilizations that no longer exist.

Some civilizations are unaware that other civilizations exist.

Some populations may actively conceal their existence.

Some occupy planes that make ordinary observation or traversal extremely difficult.

Therefore:

> **Existence does not imply discoverability.**

And:

> **One civilization's cosmological map is not automatically the true map of existence.**

---

## 6. Hidden and Isolated Civilizations

A civilization may exist in:

- a concealed physical territory;
- a hidden astral layer;
- a unique spiritual plane;
- an isolated objective realm;
- a pocket or protected domain;
- a specialized negative-spectrum environment;
- a plane accessible only through a particular traversal methodology;
- a deliberately concealed civilization-scale structure.

Some can remain unknown for extremely long periods.

---

## 7. Extinct Civilizations and Remnants

Not every civilization encountered in supernatural history still exists.

The world contains:

- destroyed civilizations;
- abandoned realms;
- ruined cities;
- dead supernatural populations;
- surviving remnants;
- displaced refugees;
- inherited artifacts;
- abandoned infrastructure;
- broken traversal systems;
- residual spiritual ecosystems;
- civilizations known only through historical evidence.

A destroyed civilization can still influence the present.

Its artifacts, descendants, ruins, laws, enemies, techniques or unresolved political consequences may survive.

---

## 8. Human Relationships

Humanity does not occupy one fixed position relative to supernatural civilizations.

Relationships can range across:

- coexistence;
- diplomacy;
- secrecy;
- symbiosis;
- protection;
- exploitation;
- worship;
- indifference;
- predation;
- conflict;
- political dependence;
- mutually enforced boundaries.

Some supernatural civilizations maintain equilibrium with humanity because humans are politically, ecologically or strategically important.

Others may have no meaningful reason to care about humanity.

Others may regard humans as negligible.

---

## 9. Civilizational Power Distance

The difference between civilizations can be enormous.

Some supernatural populations operate close enough to humanity that:

- human politics matter;
- secrecy matters;
- individual humans matter;
- diplomacy matters;
- coexistence is practical.

Other civilizations or entities are so far beyond ordinary human scale that individual humans may be treated as **effectively negligible**, comparable to insects from the perspective of the civilization's capabilities and worldview.

This is not a universal attitude of all supernatural beings.

It is a consequence of **extreme civilizational disparity**.

---

## 10. Intelligence and Philosophy

Supernatural civilizations should not be written as one shared psychology.

They can possess radically different:

- models of intelligence;
- philosophies;
- moral systems;
- aesthetics;
- concepts of beauty;
- concepts of personhood;
- political theories;
- social structures;
- attitudes toward death;
- attitudes toward humans;
- interpretations of reality;
- approaches to knowledge.

An entity can be extremely intelligent while having a philosophy that humans find incomprehensible.

Another civilization can possess sophisticated metaphysics while being politically primitive.

Another can possess extraordinary technology/magic while maintaining a culture that appears archaic.

**Capability, intelligence, culture and morality are separate variables.**

---

## 11. Devils

Devils are **one supernatural population among many**.

They should not automatically be treated as:

- the definition of demons;
- the entirety of the negative spectrum;
- the universal demon hierarchy;
- the oldest negative civilization;
- the strongest negative civilization.

Their own specific ontology and SIN-feeding mechanics remain governed by established Devil canon.

Devils participate in a much larger supernatural ecosystem.

---

## 12. Terminological Layering

The following terms may refer to different things depending on speaker and context:

### Demon
Broad human-facing or supernatural classification.

### Demonic Entity
Descriptive category that does not necessarily identify civilization.

### Demonology
Human/supernatural knowledge tradition concerned with demonic entities.

### Nether
May identify a realm, civilization, ecological category or broad cultural term depending on context.

### Abyss / Abyssal
May refer to a realm, ecological condition, civilization, species or classification.

### Rift
May refer to a traversal phenomenon, region, realm, ecological structure or population depending on context.

### Inferno / Hell
May identify specific realms or traditions rather than a single universal destination.

These terms must not automatically be collapsed into one place.

---

## 13. Political Fragmentation

The negative spectrum can contain multiple overlapping political structures.

There can be:

- empires;
- kingdoms;
- city-states;
- federations;
- clans;
- houses;
- tribes;
- military orders;
- religious institutions;
- independent territories;
- non-state civilizations;
- nomadic populations;
- ancient political remnants.

There is no requirement for a single supreme negative-spectrum government.

---

## 14. Cosmological Blind Spots

Even powerful supernatural civilizations can have incomplete knowledge.

A civilization can be:

- unaware of another civilization;
- unaware of an entire plane;
- unaware of an extinct civilization's surviving remnants;
- unable to access a particular realm;
- unable to interpret another ontology;
- politically isolated;
- intentionally excluded from wider supernatural networks.

This creates genuine unknowns rather than merely giving every faction secret knowledge of everything.

---

## 15. Worldbuilding Consequence

When an entity is introduced as a “demon,” that is **not sufficient information to determine its civilization, power, culture, philosophy, realm, political affiliation or ontology.**

The classification is an entry point.

The world behind the classification can be enormous.

---

## 16. Canon Boundary

This document does not establish a complete census of supernatural civilizations.

The exact number, names, territories, populations, alliances and ontologies of individual civilizations should be established only when canonically introduced or researched.

Unknown civilizations remain **UNKNOWN**, rather than being invented to fill gaps.

---

## 17. One-Sentence Canon

> **The supernatural world contains numerous partially connected civilizations and populations, including dozens to hundreds associated with broad negative-spectrum classifications; Devils are only one such population, while others may coexist with humans, dominate them, remain hidden on unique planes, exist in isolated realms, or survive only as remnants of destroyed civilizations, and no single demonological classification provides a complete map of this world.**
