## 1. HARD INVARIANTS

**Demon:** broad supernatural classification/ecology, not one species and not synonymous with Devil. Demons fundamentally feed on negative energy, but different demonic populations can consume different forms/sources and have different architectures, needs, instincts, and capabilities.

**Devil:** one specific dominant/higher-order demonic population/subtype, not all demons. Defining ontological tendency: **Termination / Ender / consumption of potential / apocalyptic finality.** Devils feed on SIN-associated negative energy and negative emotional energy.

**Negative Potential:** the polar opposite of positive potential. It is not misery, suffering, negative emotion, or negative energy, and it is not a separate fuel system. It is part of the deeper potential ecology underlying Devil predation.

**Devil targeting:** the important variable is trajectory/rise, not static misery. Someone already permanently miserable or effectively ended may have little meaningful positive trajectory left to terminate. Someone whose life is rising through confidence, success, relationships, ambition, recovery, spiritual/psychological development, status, or purpose can represent substantially more potential to end.

**Angel:** broad classification, not one simple race or uniform physiology. Broad positive ecological tendency: continuation and reinforcement of positive potential. Angels do not simply eat positive emotions; they can inspire, reinforce, guide, protect, stabilize, or otherwise increase positive development.

**Guardian / positive continuation:** guardian-type angelic existence can involve guidance, protection, luck/favorable outcomes, positive emotional development, reinforcement of beneficial trajectories, and metaphysical support. Guardian angel is not automatically one Biblical species/order.

**Fallen Angel:** not simply an evil angel. A fall is a fundamental inversion of function/relationship for an angelic architecture capable of falling. Documented tendency: **Continuation / torment / anguish / prolongation of negative states.** A Fallen Angel can consume negative energy from humans and remains distinct from both Devils and broad demons.

## 2. THREE MAJOR ECOLOGICAL DIRECTIONS
**Angelic / Guardian:** continuation and reinforcement of positive potential.
**Fallen Angel:** continuation / prolongation of negative states.
**Devil:** termination / ending / destruction or consumption of potential.
These are ontological/ecological tendencies, not behavioral prisons.

## 3. ENERGY VS ONTOLOGY
Never collapse the resource layer into the ontological-function layer.

Resource examples: negative energy, SIN-associated energy, other supernatural resources, Aimor pools.
Function examples: continuation, reinforcement, torment/prolongation, termination/finality, consumption of potential.
A resource does not automatically define an entity's entire ontology. An ontological tendency is not automatically one fuel type.

## 4. POTENTIAL POLARITY
**Positive Potential ↔ Negative Potential**
Do not redefine this as a simple emotion meter. Positive potential is not merely happiness. Negative potential is not merely misery. The exact mathematical/metaphysical formalism is not defined beyond the author's established relationship. Do not invent one.

## 5. DEVIL RISE PRINCIPLE
The author's core analogy is a rise-sensitive reward loop. The meaningful feature is the rise, not a constant quantity of negative stimulus.
Conceptually: **low → rising → rising → rising → collapse** is more significant than **low → low → low → low** because the first contains an active trajectory and more meaningful potential to terminate.
This is an analogy for ecological logic, not a claim that Devils literally possess human dopamine circuitry.

## 6. ALREADY ENDED PRINCIPLE
A person can reach a condition where their positive trajectory has effectively collapsed: luck no longer meaningfully turns in their favor; people reject them; positive opportunities fail to connect; blessings can pass over them without meaningful reinforcement; angelic/guardian influence fails to sustain their trajectory; their life has become effectively terminal in the relevant ecological sense.
That person may remain extremely miserable. **Misery alone does not make them a high-value Devil target.** If there is no meaningful rise left to terminate, the Devil can seek another person.

## 7. BROADER DEMON ECOLOGY
The demon category is intentionally enormous. Different demons can consume different negative-energy types, consume energy produced by their own kind, have specialized constitutions, feed specific pools rather than restoring all capability, and possess radically different physiologies. Demons can be spiritual, parasitic, territorial, folkloric, godlike, primordial, or otherwise anomalous. There can be many demon populations/civilizations, including ones vastly beyond humanity.

## 8. ANGELIC ECOLOGY
Angels are likewise broad. Categories can differ in physiology, embodiment, energy pools, duties, hierarchy, specialization, metaphysical architecture, and combat/non-combat role. Not every angel is a fighter. Guardian, guide, specialized worker, living weapon, nonhuman embodiment, speechless entity, artificial/constructed existence, and abstract/metaphysical forms can exist. Do not reduce angels to the familiar DxD cast or a single Biblical template.

## 9. FALLEN-ANGEL ECOLOGY
A Fallen Angel is an inverted angelic architecture where the relevant order is capable of falling. The inversion can affect function, energy relationship, emotional/ecological influence, supernatural imprint, feeding, abilities, immunities, and weaknesses. A powerful Fallen Angel can retain a powerful underlying angelic architecture while becoming dangerous through inversion. Fallen angels primarily feeding on negative energy from humans is an established distinction from broader demons that may feed from many sources.

## 10. SUPERNATURAL IMPRINT
**Angelic:** continuation / reinforcement / favorable development.
**Fallen Angel:** suffering continues — repetition, dependence, degradation, cycles, captivity, prolongation.
**Devil:** something has been ended — finality, irreversible loss, catastrophic compression, destruction of possibility.
These are signatures/tendencies, not rigid action restrictions.

## 11. INSTINCT VS INDIVIDUAL PSYCHOLOGY
**Ontology supplies pressure. Psychology interprets it. Agency chooses behavior.**
A Devil can preserve something. A Fallen Angel can kill something. An Angel can fail. Individual exceptions do not erase underlying ontological tendencies.
