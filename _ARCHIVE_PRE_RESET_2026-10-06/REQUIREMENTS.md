# Requirements Register

Living register of confirmed project requirements and constraints.

## Confirmed

### Product
- EverWorlds is a large-scale React-based extension for SillyTavern.
- It heavily modifies SillyTavern's properties and behavior.
- It adds extra features and capabilities.
- It has a specific internal roleplay structure.

### Core Objective
- The project's true/core goal is solving basic and fundamental LLM issues that interfere with high-quality roleplay.
- The project must account for LLM limitations, tendencies, and failure patterns.
- The system is intended to actively prevent or mitigate those problems.

### Output Quality
- Outputs are generally envisioned as highly detailed digital twins of worlds, people, or other subjects.
- Psychological realism is a key quality target.
- The project is intended to be professional-tier.

### Finale
- A Finale must preserve the identity and defining traits of the original world.
- A Finale must evolve the original world deeply rather than merely applying surface-level improvements.
- The resulting world may be substantially different in quality, depth, sophistication, and complexity while remaining recognizably the original world.

### Production
- The Scheme of Work is a project productivity/production framework, not a school or teaching document.
- It exists to help the project reach its end goal while reducing burnout, disorder, messes, and inconsistencies.
- Work is organized conceptually as **Project → Phase → Task**.
- Work is planned and tracked on a daily basis.
- The working participants are the user and AI agents; no external team is assumed.
- A weekly review is part of the framework.

## Not Yet Defined

Internal mechanics, detailed architecture, implementation design, and other technical specifics remain open until deliberately established.
