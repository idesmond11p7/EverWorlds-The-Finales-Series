# Chapter 6 — Function Map

## Status
**LOCKED production control**

Chapter 6 is an exceptional multi-function chapter. The travel sequence is its physical spine, not its complete purpose.

This map translates the established Chapter 6 intent into production functions without adding new plot.

## 1. Primary Function — Make the New Life Inhabitable

The reader must stop experiencing Jonah/Ira's situation as a temporary information problem and begin experiencing it as a life he is physically occupying.

Implementation:
- apartment use becomes increasingly automatic;
- clothing, food, documents, money, route, and belongings are handled through action;
- public movement becomes possible;
- Kuoh becomes traversable rather than merely named;
- the Academy becomes a real institution he physically enters.

Failure:
If removing the travel details leaves only “he went to school,” the chapter has failed its primary function.

## 2. Psychological Function — Show the Cycle, Not Explain It

Chapter 6 continues the psychological state established in Chapter 5 without repeating Chapter 5's collapse.

The chapter should show:
- residual emotional weight;
- normalization through routine;
- curiosity returning;
- small moments of frustration;
- competence producing confidence;
- environmental absorption;
- anticipation at the threshold.

Do not repeatedly tell the reader that Jonah is recovering, depressed, optimistic, or adapting. Those states should be legible through attention, choices, reactions, self-talk, mistakes, and behavior.

## 3. Body / Mind Function — Integration Without Memory Transfer

Jonah remains Jonah.

Ira's memories do not arrive.

The body can nevertheless become less alien through:
- automatic movement;
- physical habits;
- clothing/body awareness;
- changes in ease or awkwardness;
- reactions that Jonah notices without fully understanding.

The chapter should make the body increasingly usable without turning this into an explicit supernatural lecture.

## 4. Ability Function — Establish Operation Through Lived Context

Any ability material already established by higher-authority canon may be demonstrated or clarified through Jonah's actual experience where the scene naturally permits it.

Do not:
- invent a new power;
- invent a universal energy system;
- dump a power catalogue into Chapter 6;
- reveal the nature of the later power plotline prematurely.

Chapter 6 prepares the reader to understand Jonah as a person who possesses abilities; Chapter 8 owns the first actual power plotline.

If a specific ability/mechanic is not currently established in authoritative project material, mark it UNKNOWN rather than inventing it.

## 5. World-Scale Function — Kuoh as a Real Place Before It Is a Supernatural Place

The reader needs a physical model of Kuoh before the hidden ecology becomes important.

Build scale through:
- rail distance;
- density changes;
- architecture;
- roads;
- vegetation;
- commerce;
- school traffic;
- ordinary residents;
- institutional movement.

Do not reveal the supernatural explanation for those patterns.

The reader should be able to believe Kuoh exists independently of Jonah.

## 6. Sona Function — Presence Before Explanation

Sona's Chapter 6 function is not exposition.

She is a first unexplained proximity:
- visually/concretely present;
- ordinary enough to be explainable;
- memorable through behavior or social positioning;
- not identified by Jonah as important;
- not framed through supernatural aura;
- not given authorial lore through first-person narration.

The asymmetry matters: the author may know who she is; Jonah does not.

## 7. Social Function — Jonah Learns to Exist as Ira

The chapter should contain ordinary interactions where nobody knows Jonah's previous life.

This establishes:
- public anonymity;
- practical identity;
- social competence;
- how other people respond to Ira;
- the difference between internal crisis and external normality.

The ordinary world should not constantly react to him as extraordinary.

## 8. Narrative Function — Build the Threshold

The chapter moves from:

**private survival**
→ **ordinary routine**
→ **public competence**
→ **physical entry into Kuoh Academy**
→ **examination threshold**

The 9:00 AM bell is therefore a structural handoff, not merely a timestamp.

## 9. Reader Model Function

By the bell, the reader should have a stable mental model of:
- where Jonah/Ira came from that morning;
- how he traveled;
- what the surrounding environment physically felt like;
- where the Academy sits within that journey;
- how Jonah behaves in ordinary public life;
- what has changed psychologically since the prior chapter;
- who Sona appears to be from Jonah's limited perspective;
- what remains unexplained.

## 10. Rendering-Mode Map

Different functions may use different prose modes.

### Apartment
**Quiet environmental habitation + psychological interiority**

### Preparation / route
**Conventional narrative movement + grounded perceptual rendering**

### Station
**Immersive physical/logistical rendering**

### Train
**Immersive perceptual rendering + reflective interiority**

### Food / public interaction
**Dialogue-forward movement + embodied detail**

### Navigation error
**Action/event rendering**

### Kuoh approach
**RTX-style perceptual/environmental rendering**

### Academy registration
**Dialogue-forward institutional realism**

### Sona glimpse
**Selective perceptual focus without interpretive exposition**

### Waiting
**Quiet environmental habitation + anticipatory interiority**

### Bell
**Action/event threshold rendering**

These modes can coexist. No single prose mode is mandatory for the entire chapter.

## 11. Hard Anti-Failure Conditions

STOP rather than continue if a draft:
- turns the chapter into a travel montage;
- treats the apartment as a launchpad;
- repeats Chapter 5's identity/body discovery;
- explains Jonah's psychology instead of dramatizing it;
- makes the body disappear from the prose;
- makes Sona obviously important to Jonah;
- leaks supernatural knowledge;
- invents unsupported Ira history;
- invents unsupported geography;
- converts abilities into a generic power-system exposition;
- reduces the world to descriptive inventory;
- uses paragraph merging to disguise the old reporting architecture;
- reaches the exam before 9:00 AM;
- starts the examination before Chapter 7.

## 12. Definition of Success

Chapter 6 succeeds when the reader reaches the bell with the feeling that Jonah/Ira has **actually lived a morning in this world**, while simultaneously carrying forward psychological, bodily, social, geographic, institutional, and hidden-world foundations.

The chapter should make the later extraordinary material matter more because the ordinary world has first become concrete.
