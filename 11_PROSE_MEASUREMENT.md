## Typography audit
Record long captions, isolated words, unusual Unicode, gargantuan words, deliberate capitalization, fragments, visual spacing, and other nonstandard typography as measurable devices.

## Jaggedness
Jaggedness means meaningful variation in token/sentence/paragraph structure, not random fragmentation.

## Release failure
If readability, spatial clarity, typography, cadence, or information delivery materially interferes with comprehension, the prose fails regardless of stylistic ambition.

## Evidence rule
Metrics must be calculated from the actual manuscript under review. Never infer them from memory or a description.
