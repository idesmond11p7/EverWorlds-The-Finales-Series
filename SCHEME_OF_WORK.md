# EverWorlds Scheme of Work

## Purpose

The Scheme of Work is the project's productivity and production framework. It exists to help EverWorlds reach its end goal without emotional burnout, disorder, messes, or inconsistencies interfering with progress.

This is **not** a school or teaching document.

## Work Model

**Project → Phase → Task**

Work is organized by project phase and then by concrete task, with daily planning and tracking.

## Areas of Work

The framework must accommodate work that emerges across:

1. Research
2. Writing / Story / Narrative / Episodes
3. Visual development
4. Production
5. Marketing / Presentation
6. Documentation
7. Other work as it emerges

## Participants

Work is performed by:

- The user
- AI agents

No external team structure is assumed unless the project later establishes one.

## Review Cadence

A **weekly review** is part of the production framework.

## Workflow

The exact lifecycle is still to be finalized. A useful working model may include stages such as capture, definition, planning, active work, review, approval, and completion, with exception states for blocked, paused, revision, cancellation, or archival work.

These lifecycle states are currently a **proposal**, not a locked requirement.

## Operating Principle

The Scheme of Work should reduce cognitive and organizational load rather than create another source of overhead. It should make the next meaningful piece of work clear while preserving enough structure to maintain consistency across a large, evolving project.
