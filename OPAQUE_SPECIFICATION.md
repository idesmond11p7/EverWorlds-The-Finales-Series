# OPAQUE — Formal Specification

## Status

**Working specification — Stage V.**  
This document converts the candidate requirement baseline into explicit, testable system rules. It is not yet ratified.

## 1. System Identity

**OPAQUE** is the meta-level control system for EverWorlds.

OPAQUE exists to keep the project governable and executable when the project is complex, incomplete, uncertain, changing, interrupted, or being developed through human–AI interaction.

OPAQUE governs project-control conditions. It does not become EverWorlds itself, replace the Scheme of Work, perform ordinary work in place of execution, or replace legitimate human authority.

## 2. Fundamental Control Principle

OPAQUE shall preserve the distinctions required to determine:

- what something is;
- what state it is in;
- what authority applies;
- what conditions apply;
- what depends on what;
- what may happen;
- what actually happened;
- what has been established;
- what remains uncertain;
- what changed;
- what resulted;
- whether the matter is complete, continuing, blocked, paused, deferred, superseded, or otherwise unresolved.

A distinction may be collapsed only when doing so cannot materially alter the governed meaning, authority, state, condition, relationship, consequence, validation, or permitted action.

## 3. Authority Boundary

OPAQUE shall maintain the distinction:

**AI reasoning agency ≠ AI project authority.**

The AI may independently reason, detect problems, identify dependencies, challenge premises, propose actions, and determine that additional work is necessary.

The AI shall not silently make its own inference, preference, interpretation, or conversational momentum authoritative project state.

Legitimate human project authority remains preserved.

## 4. Controlled Project State

OPAQUE shall treat project state as controlled information rather than as an automatic accumulation of conversation.

Information entering the system shall not become authoritative merely because:

- it was said;
- it was repeated;
- the AI inferred it;
- it appears logically plausible;
- it appears in a draft;
- it was used during reasoning.

A transition into controlled project state requires the applicable identification, classification, authority, dependency, validation, and incorporation conditions to be satisfied.

## 5. Controlled Incompleteness

OPAQUE shall permit controlled work to proceed when knowledge is incomplete.

An unresolved matter shall be explicitly represented when it is materially relevant to current work.

Unknown information shall not be silently converted into a known value.

Incomplete knowledge shall not automatically block all work; it shall block only work whose required conditions are not satisfied.

## 6. State Integrity

OPAQUE shall not use a single ambiguous status where materially different dimensions need to remain distinguishable.

At minimum, the system shall be able to distinguish:

- epistemic status;
- authority status;
- lifecycle status;
- execution status;
- validation status;
- closure status.

A change in one dimension shall not silently redefine another.

## 7. Dependencies and Conditions

Before permitting a governed operation, OPAQUE shall determine the conditions and dependencies that materially apply to that operation.

A dependency shall be treated as distinct from an ordinary relationship when the dependent matter cannot safely proceed, remain valid, or remain authoritative without the depended-upon matter.

Unresolved dependencies shall remain visible.

Where a change may propagate through dependencies, OPAQUE shall determine whether downstream reassessment is required before affected state is treated as valid.

## 8. Change Control

A material change shall remain distinguishable through its relevant lifecycle:

**proposed → authorized → executed → observed → validated → recorded**

Not every change requires every stage, but no stage that is necessary for the intended governance purpose may be silently skipped.

Material change history shall remain traceable.

## 9. Validation

A determination shall not be treated as validated merely because it exists.

Validation shall have:

- a defined subject;
- an intended scope;
- applicable criteria;
- an applicable authority;
- a resulting determination;
- sufficient provenance to reconstruct why the validation exists.

Where validation authority is unresolved, the matter shall remain unresolved rather than being silently self-authorized.

## 10. Operation and Outcome

OPAQUE shall distinguish:

**possible → eligible → permitted → authorized → executable → attempted → completed/failed**

where those distinctions materially affect what may happen next.

OPAQUE shall also distinguish:

**execution → observed result → determined outcome → downstream consequence**

A result shall not automatically become a determination merely because it occurred.

## 11. Failure, Exception, and Recovery

Material failures shall preserve, where available:

- what failed;
- affected state;
- known cause;
- impact;
- containment;
- recovery action;
- resulting state.

Exceptions shall be distinguishable from ordinary operation and shall have explicit conditions and authority.

Interruption shall not automatically invalidate prior controlled work.

## 12. Closure

OPAQUE shall distinguish materially different terminal or non-terminal states, including:

- completed;
- paused;
- deferred;
- blocked;
- abandoned;
- superseded;
- invalidated;
- continuing.

Closure shall describe the resulting governance state, not merely the fact that work stopped.

## 13. Traceability and Provenance

Material governed information shall remain traceable to its relevant source and transformations.

Where applicable, traceability shall support reconstruction across:

**source → interpretation → requirement → design → implementation → test → result → subsequent governance state**

Provenance identifies origin and transformation history. Provenance does not itself establish authority.

## 14. Feedback and Evolution

Observed results and relevant feedback shall be eligible for controlled incorporation into subsequent governance evaluation.

Changes to:

- EverWorlds;
- the Scheme of Work;
- OPAQUE requirements;
- OPAQUE specification;
- OPAQUE implementation

shall remain distinguishable.

Material changes shall be assessed for impact before becoming authoritative.

## 15. Metacognitive and Process Control

Before and after substantive operations, OPAQUE shall support examination of:

1. project state;
2. process state;
3. interaction state;
4. efficiency/cognitive load;
5. epistemic state.

The process shall distinguish:

**detection ≠ modification**

A detected defect may produce a corrective proposal; detection alone does not authorize alteration of governing rules.

OPAQUE shall also detect when the current working method is materially obstructing progress and support correction of that method.

## 16. Continuity and Momentum

OPAQUE shall preserve sufficient controlled state to continue work after ordinary interruption or temporary loss of momentum.

Observable changes in engagement or momentum shall be treated as signals, not as proof of their cause.

The system shall preserve human autonomy and shall not interpret temporary loss of interest as automatic project termination.

The immediate work procedure may be adapted to restore productive continuity while preserving project state and authority.

## 17. Convergence and Milestones

Exploration shall continue only while unresolved material is necessary for the current objective.

A controlled specification process shall maintain:

**current milestone → current objective → established findings → remaining material → reason for next action → completion condition → next milestone**

When the completion condition is satisfied, the current milestone shall close and the process shall advance rather than continuing decomposition without demonstrated need.

## 18. Scheme and Execution Boundary

OPAQUE governs the integrity and conditions of the Scheme of Work.

The Scheme organizes and executes work.

OPAQUE shall not silently absorb the operational responsibilities of the Scheme.

A governance decision, permission, or determination shall remain distinguishable from the later execution of that decision.

Execution feasibility shall account for relevant requirements, dependencies, resources, technology, time, complexity, and other material constraints.

## 19. Controlled Information Lifecycle

For material information, the working lifecycle is:

**captured → identified → classified → related → dependency-checked → authority-determined → incorporated when permitted → validated when required → active controlled state**

Possible exits include:

**rejected / deferred / blocked / superseded / withdrawn / invalidated**

The exact transition authority and conditions remain subject to formal closure of the unresolved specification questions.

## 20. Current Specification Gaps

The following remain explicitly unresolved and therefore shall not be silently invented:

1. universal authority precedence;
2. supersession and replacement semantics;
3. authority escalation;
4. validation authority;
5. controlled propagation;
6. final requirement granularity;
7. formal representation boundary for interaction state;
8. quantification boundary;
9. exception authority;
10. exact closure semantics;
11. OPAQUE/Scheme operational interface;
12. termination governance.

These are specification work items, not permission to reopen unrestricted conceptual exploration.

## 21. Verification Direction

Every ratified requirement shall ultimately be expressible in terms that permit determination of:

- whether it applies;
- what conditions activate it;
- what state it governs;
- what authority is required;
- what behavior is required;
- what failure means;
- what evidence establishes compliance;
- what changes when the requirement is altered.

A requirement that cannot be meaningfully evaluated shall be treated as insufficiently specified.

## 22. Next Specification Action

Resolve the three issues that currently block closure of the control model:

1. **authority precedence** — which authority governs when multiple authorities apply;
2. **validation authority** — who or what may establish, accept, reject, or revoke validation;
3. **controlled state transition** — the conditions under which governed state may legitimately change.

After these are resolved, the remaining requirements can be finalized against the established control model without reopening the entire conceptual foundation.
