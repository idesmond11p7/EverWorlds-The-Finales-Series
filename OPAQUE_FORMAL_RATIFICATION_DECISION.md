## 4. Decision Requirements

Before a ratified state is recorded, the authorized decision must establish:

1. which provisional D-01 dispositions are accepted;
2. whether any D-01 wording requires amendment;
3. whether any provisional D-03 item remains deferred;
4. whether any unresolved U-item is explicitly resolved;
5. the resulting ratified specification version;
6. the authority and date of the decision;
7. any required amendments and their traceability;
8. the effective boundary between ratified and still-provisional material.

## 5. No Silent Authorization

The following do not constitute ratification:

- the completion of Pass 8;
- the AI's provisional recommendations;
- documentation of those recommendations;
- the user's instruction to continue the project;
- logical consistency;
- implementation dependency;
- elapsed review time.

An explicit authorization event is required.
